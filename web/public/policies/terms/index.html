Using Files Format

Files Format provides browser-based tools for ordinary PDF, image, text and data tasks, plus optional temporary sharing. Use the service only for files and content you have the right to access and share. You are responsible for checking results before relying on them.

Link Tool and QR Tool

These tools upload one file or pasted text (up to 10 MB) to a temporary storage service and generate a public-by-link download URL. Anyone who obtains the URL can download the content. Links expire after seven days and objects are scheduled for automatic deletion. Do not upload private, confidential, personal or unlawful content. Do not upload content you lack permission to distribute. You remain responsible for the content you upload and share.

Files Format may reject an upload if the security check, storage service or free usage allowance is unavailable. The service does not guarantee permanent availability of a share URL. Keep your own original copy.

Keep original copies

Processing may change document structure, orientation, metadata or image quality. Keep an original copy of important documents. Do not rely on Files Format as the only copy of an important file.

Limits and compatibility

Supported formats and safety limits are shown in the app and on each tool page. Encrypted PDFs, OCR, editing existing PDF text, digital signatures, form filling, annotation editing and animated image processing are not supported.

No guarantee

The tools are provided as-is. Not every file, browser or device will necessarily be supported. Browser memory and device performance can affect processing. Verify all output before using it for important purposes.

These services are subject to Cloudflare terms and privacy policy. Third-party libraries are subject to their own terms and licenses, as described in the licensing notices.
