Privacy Policy

Last updated: 23 November 2026.

Local processing

PDF, image and ordinary text/data processing takes place in your browser. Files selected for these local tools are not uploaded to a Files Format processing server.

Optional Link Tool and QR Tool sharing

When you choose Link Tool or QR Tool, the one file or pasted text you select is uploaded to Cloudflare R2 so Files Format can create a shareable download URL. Uploads are limited to 10 MB and require a Cloudflare Turnstile security check. The generated URL is public to anyone who has it and expires after seven days. Objects are scheduled for deletion by the R2 lifecycle rule, normally within 24 hours after expiry. Do not upload confidential, personal, financial, medical or otherwise sensitive content. Do not upload material you do not have permission to distribute.

Cloudflare receives the upload bytes and standard request metadata and processes them under its service terms. The share service does not require an account and does not expose an upload directory or search endpoint. The random share URL is the access credential; anyone with it can download the content.

Website hosting

The static website and sharing API are hosted by Cloudflare Pages and Cloudflare R2. Cloudflare may receive standard website request metadata, such as IP address, browser and request information, to deliver and secure the site. See Cloudflare's Privacy Policy for details.

Offline storage

After a successful visit, a service worker can cache static application files for offline use. The service worker deliberately does not cache upload API requests or public share downloads.

Analytics, advertising and accounts

The application does not include analytics scripts, advertising integrations, account sign-in or an AI API.

Your choices

You can clear the site's cached files in your browser's site settings. That removes the offline app copy; it does not delete original documents stored elsewhere on your device.

Changes

This policy may change if the site's hosting or processing behavior changes. The date above will be updated when material changes are made.
