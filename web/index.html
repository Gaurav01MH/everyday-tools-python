    Privacy and usage limits

    Local tools: PDF, image and text files are processed in your browser. They are not uploaded to a Files Format processing server.

    Link Tool and QR Tool: these are the exceptions. The file or text you select is uploaded to create a public-by-link download. Anyone with the URL can download it. Links expire after 7 days. Do not share sensitive content.

    Current limits

    • 100 MB total input; 50 MB per PDF.
    • 20 MB and 16 megapixels per image.
    • 20 files per batch; 250 PDF pages (100 for PDF previews/rendering).
    • 100 MB maximum generated PDF or ZIP file.
    • Share uploads: one item, up to 10 MB, 7-day expiry (only on a configured share host).

    Encrypted PDFs, OCR, editing existing PDF text, signatures, forms and animated images are not supported. PDF edits may not preserve bookmarks, form fields, annotations or digital signatures. Always check your exported file.

    QR Tool for private text/URLs generates a QR code locally. The sharing QR option first uploads the selected content to create its link.
