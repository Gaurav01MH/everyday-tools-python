    Privacy and usage limits

    PDF and image editing tools work locally. Link Tool and QR Tool are different: when you choose them, one file or pasted text is uploaded to the Files Format share service to create a public-by-link download.

    Shared uploads are stored in the Files Format R2 bucket, protected by a security check, and automatically expire after seven days. Anyone with the share URL can download them. Do not upload confidential or personal documents. Cloudflare receives the upload and standard request metadata.

    To keep browser memory use reasonable, this version limits total selected input to 100 MB, each PDF to 50 MB, each image to 20 MB and 16 megapixels, a batch to 20 files, and PDF processing to 250 pages per document (100 pages for PNG rendering). Generated PDF and ZIP downloads are limited to 100 MB. Large or unusual documents may still exceed your browser's memory.

    Encrypted PDFs, OCR, PDF text editing, signatures, and form filling are not supported. PDF page-copy operations can discard document-level items such as bookmarks, attachments, form fields, and annotations; saving a changed PDF also invalidates existing digital signatures. Image conversion depends on formats your browser can decode; animated images are not supported.

    QR Tool generates the QR image in your browser from the share URL. The QR image is not sent to an external QR service. Shared content is removed after seven days; preserve your own original copy.
